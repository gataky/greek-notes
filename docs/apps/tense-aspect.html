Where you slip

    Things to watch
    • Every verb has two stems. The continuous one (γράφ-) is for an action in progress or one that happens again and again. The simple one (γράψ-) is for one action, seen as complete.
    • In the past: έγραφα is "I was writing" or "I used to write"; έγραψα is "I wrote" (once, and it's done).
    • In the future: θα γράφω is "I'll be writing" or "I'll write (every day)"; θα γράψω is "I'll write (it, once)".
    • After θέλω να, μπορώ να, προτιμώ να: να γράψω for one action, which is most of the time; να γράφω for a habit or an ability (ξέρεις να κολυμπάς;).
    • The present only has the continuous stem: γράφω is both "I write" and "I'm writing".
    • Clue words for the continuous: κάθε πρωί, κάθε Σάββατο, συνήθως, συχνά, πάντα, and a background action with όταν ("I was reading when you phoned"). For the simple: χθες, αύριο, απόψε, αμέσως, αυτή τη φορά, when the action happens once.
    • Object pronouns and δεν / μην go between θα or να and the verb: θα το γράψω, να μην το πεις.
    • A few verbs have one form for both aspects (κάνω, περιμένω, σερβίρω, προσφέρω), and έχω and ξέρω have no simple stem at all.
