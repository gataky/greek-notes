Aspect Forks

How each verb's present forks into its two stems, and how each stem makes a past and a future.

Every verb has two stems. The imperfective stem is the present stem and shows an action as ongoing or repeated. The perfective stem shows it as one complete event. Verbs are grouped by what turns one stem into the other.

Past
(augment) + stem + -α
Future
θα + stem + -ω
Augment
The past puts the stress on the third syllable from the end. If a verb is too short for that, it adds έ- (έγραψα). Longer verbs just move the stress (διάβασα).
