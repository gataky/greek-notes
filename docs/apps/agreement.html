    Where you slip

      Things to watch
      • The article and the adjective take the noun's gender, which the noun's ending doesn't always show: ο μεγάλος άντρας, η μεγάλη οδός, το μεγάλο όνομα.
      • The adjective's ending often differs from the noun's: τον καλό πατέρα, της ωραίας πόλης. Match the gender, number and case, not the letters.
      • την keeps its ν before a vowel and before κ, π, τ, ξ, ψ: την καλή, την άσχημη, but τη μεγάλη.
      • Neuter nominative and accusative are always the same. The genitive plural is των for every gender.
      • Some nouns have no genitive plural in use. The drill leaves those out.
