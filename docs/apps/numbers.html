    Marking

    Where you slip

      Things to watch
      • Only 1, 3, 4 (and 13, 14, 21, 23…), the hundreds from 200 and χίλιοι change with the noun: τρεις γυναίκες, τρία παιδιά, διακόσιοι άντρες. Every other number has one form.
      • On its own, for counting, prices and phone numbers, a number is neuter: τρία, τέσσερα, διακόσια.
      • εκατό becomes εκατόν before another number: εκατόν ένα. There's no "one" before 100 or 1000: χίλια.
      • From 2000 up, thousands are the feminine noun χιλιάδες, so the number before it is feminine whatever you count: τρεις χιλιάδες παιδιά.
      • In compounds the "one" stays singular, and so does nothing else: είκοσι ένας άντρες.
      • Spelling traps: τρεις but τέσσερις; δεκατρείς has an accent, τρεις doesn't. See Numerals.
