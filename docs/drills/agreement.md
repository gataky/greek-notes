# Agreement

Fill the gap with the article, an adjective and a noun from your word list, all in the noun's gender and in the case the sentence needs: nominative for the subject, accusative for the object, genitive for "of". Chips turn each case and number on or off, and the adjective too. The forms are the dictionary's.

See [Articles](../grammar/articles.md) and [Adjectives](../grammar/adjectives.md) for the notes behind this drill, or [open the drill on its own page](../apps/agreement.html).
