# Active or passive

Present tense. Each English sentence uses a verb from your list that has both an active and a -μαι form, or a deponent that only has the -μαι form. Type the Greek verb: choose the voice, then match the ending to the subject. Pairs come back to back (the same verb once active, once -μαι), and the persons you miss come up more often.
