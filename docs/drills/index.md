# Greek Drills

Type the Greek for a marked word or phrase in an English sentence. Each drill brings back what you miss, and keeps its progress on this device.

| Drill | What you practise |
| --- | --- |
| [Active or passive](passive-verbs.md) | The present, choosing the active or the passive form and the person |
| [Tense and aspect](tense-aspect.md) | The present, past, future and dependent, continuous or simple |
| [Object pronouns](object-pronouns.md) | The weak pronoun and where it goes around the verb |
| [Noun gender](noun-gender.md) | A noun's article, then its plural |
| [Agreement](agreement.md) | Article, adjective and noun in one gender and case |
| [Prepositions](prepositions.md) | The preposition, article and noun, in the accusative |
| [Numbers](numbers.md) | Numbers in words, read back, and counting things |

The grammar notes and the verb tables are in [Greek Notes](../index.md), and words in the [dictionary](../dictionary/index.md): both are apps of their own.
