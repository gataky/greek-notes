# Noun gender

A noun from your word list: pick its article (ο, η or το), then type its plural with the article. The ending is the best clue, and after each answer the drill shows how often that ending goes with each gender in your list. Endings you miss come up more often. The forms are the dictionary's.

See [Articles](../grammar/articles.md) for the notes behind this drill, or [open the drill on its own page](../apps/noun-gender.html).
