# Tense and aspect

Active verbs. Each English sentence marks one verb: type it in Greek, with θα or να when the form takes one. Choose the tense (present, past, future, or after να), then the stem: continuous for something in progress or repeated, simple for one complete action. A verb's two aspects come back to back, and the tenses you miss come up more often. The forms are the ones in the [verb tables](../tools/verb-tables.md).
