# Verb groups

Active verbs from your word list. Pick the group each one is in: the rule that turns its present stem into its perfective one (γράφω, θα γράψω: a labial). With the past and future showing you work it out from the forms; turn them off to answer from memory. After each answer the verb's fork shows how its stems make the past and future. Groups you miss come up more often, and you can turn groups off to practise a few at a time.

The groups and verbs are the ones on the [aspect forks](../notes/tools/aspect-forks.md) page, with the forms the [verb tables](../notes/tools/verb-tables.md) show.
