# Object pronouns

Each English sentence marks a verb with its object, and the Greek has a gap for them: type the weak pronoun (το, τον, της, μου…) and the verb, in the right order. The pronoun goes before the verb, after θα, να, δεν and μην, but after an imperative, and with two pronouns the one for "to whom" comes first. The rules you miss come up more often.
