# Personal Pronouns

Greek has two sets. **Strong** pronouns carry their own stress and stand alone. **Weak** pronouns are short and lean on a verb or noun. Weak is the default; strong adds emphasis.

## Leave out the subject

The verb ending already shows the person, so εγώ, εσύ and the rest are only for emphasis or contrast: **Εγώ** δουλεύω κι **εσύ** κοιμάσαι (*I'm* working and *you're* sleeping). Plain "I'm working" is just Δουλεύω.

## Weak pronouns

**Accusative is the direct object, genitive is "to" someone:**

| English | Greek |
| --- | --- |
| I see him. | **Τον** βλέπω. |
| I'm talking to him. | **Του** μιλάω. |
| She gave me the keys. | **Μου** έδωσε τα κλειδιά. |
| I sent it to her. | **Της** το έστειλα. |

**Where they go:**

| English | Greek | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I know her. | **Την** ξέρω. | Before the verb |
| I don't know her. | Δεν **την** ξέρω. | After δεν, μην |
| I'll tell you. | Θα **σου** πω. | After θα, να |
| Give it to me. | Δώσ' **μου το**. | After an imperative |
| Seeing him… | Βλέποντάς **τον**… | After -οντας |
| Don't tell him. | Μην **του** το πεις. | Negative imperative: before the verb |

- **Two together: genitive first.** **Μου το** έδωσε (he gave it to me). After an imperative, δώσ' **το μου** is just as common.
- **A second accent appears** when a verb stressed on its third-last syllable takes a pronoun: διάβασ**έ** το (read it), τηλεφώνησ**έ** μου (phone me).
- **Spoken Greek often doubles the object**, repeating it as a weak pronoun: **Τον** ξέρω **τον Νίκο**. **Το** βιβλίο **το** διάβασα.
- **The nominative has weak forms too**, only after να and πού 'ν': **Να τος!** (There he is!) **Πού 'ν' τη;** (Where is she?) The rest are το, τοι, τες, τα.

**Some common verbs put the person in the genitive or accusative**, where English uses a subject:

| English | Greek |
| --- | --- |
| I like coffee. | **Μου** αρέσει ο καφές. |
| I miss you. | **Μου** λείπεις. |
| It seems to me… | **Μου** φαίνεται… |
| It suits you. | **Σου** πάει. |
| My name is Nikos. | **Με** λένε Νίκο. |

## Strong pronouns

Use them where a weak pronoun can't go, or to stress who you mean.

| English | Greek | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who? Me. | Ποιος; **Εγώ**. | Alone |
| Who did you see? Him. | Ποιον είδες; **Αυτόν**. | Alone |
| It's you I want, not him. | **Εσένα** θέλω, όχι **αυτόν**. | Contrast |
| *I* like it. | **Εμένα** μου αρέσει. | Emphasis, doubled |
| This is for you. | Αυτό είναι για **σένα**. | After a [preposition](../prepositions.md#pronouns-after-a-preposition) |
