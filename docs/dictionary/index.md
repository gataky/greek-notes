# Dictionary

Type Greek, with or without accents, Greeklish or English. Inflected forms find their dictionary word: είπα finds λέω. A word's page shows its meanings and its forms: a verb's conjugation and aspect fork, a noun's or adjective's declension.

The pin next to a word saves it, and the history button in the bar lists the saved words, so you can jump back and forth between them. The list is kept on this device.
