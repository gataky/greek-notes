# Greek Notes

Notes on Modern Greek grammar, from the articles to the passive. Pick a note from the menu.

The tools for verbs open full-page:

- [Verb tables](tools/verb-tables.md): every form of a verb, with the endings highlighted.
- [Conjugation patterns](tools/conjugation-patterns.md): how each group of verbs conjugates.
- [Aspect forks](tools/aspect-forks.md): how a verb's two stems differ, grouped by what changes.

The [dictionary](../dictionary/index.md) and the [drills](../drills/index.md) are apps of their own, all three linked from [Greek](../index.md).
