# Verb tables

Pick a verb to see all its forms: every tense and aspect in the indicative, plus the subjunctive and imperative. Use the checkboxes to show only what you're working on. The ending of each form is highlighted.

The verbs come from the words tab of the vocabulary sheet. After adding or changing verbs there, run `python3 scripts/build_verbs.py` and commit `docs/tools/verbs.json`. You can also [open the tables on their own page](verb-tables-app.html).
