# Aspect forks

Every verb has two stems: the imperfective, which is the present's (γράφ-), and the perfective (γράψ-). Each makes a past and a future. Here your verbs are grouped by what turns one stem into the other, with the letters that change highlighted. Pick a verb to see its fork. The verb tables show the same fork under every active verb's table, including verbs that aren't in your word list.

The groups and highlights are worked out from the dictionary's forms, so a verb added to the sheet finds its group on its own. Irregular verbs don't get a fork. Passive verbs are left out for now. You can also [open the forks on their own page](../apps/aspect-forks.html).
