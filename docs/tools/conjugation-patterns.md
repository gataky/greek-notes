# Conjugation patterns

How each group of verbs conjugates (a1, a2, b1, b2, c1–c4), shown with one regular verb per group. The highlighted endings are the pattern. Under each group you'll find the verbs from your list that follow it; click one to see its own forms in the [verb tables](verb-tables.md).

You can also [open the patterns on their own page](conjugation-patterns-app.html).
